A movement of care

We believe in the
power of together.

Blessed To Bless Humanity Aid was born from a simple conviction: every person deserves the chance to live with dignity, safety and hope.

Headquartered in Kinshasa, we work across the Democratic Republic of Congo, Kenya and Tanzania. Our programmes are shaped with communities, led by local knowledge and strengthened through trusted partnerships.

Our values

How we
show up.

01

Dignity first

We see the whole person, listen to lived experience and make space for people to lead their own change.

02

Local power

We work with communities, not around them, because the most lasting solutions are built close to home.

03

Trust in action

We are open about our work, accountable for our resources and committed to learning as we go.

Our philosophy
“We are blessed to bless others, supported to support others, and helped to help others.”

Our work is an invitation to turn what we have received — care, opportunity, solidarity — into a future shared by more people.
