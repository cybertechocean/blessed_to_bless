Frequently asked questions

Good questions
lead to good action.

Where does Blessed To Bless work?

We are headquartered in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of Congo, with programmes and partnerships in Kenya and Tanzania.

How can I donate?

Visit our donation page for current giving options, or contact our team for verified bank and mobile money details.

Can I volunteer or partner with you?

Yes. Share your interests through our volunteer form or contact us to explore a partnership that fits your goals.

How do you choose your programmes?

We listen to communities and work with local partners to understand needs, priorities and the solutions most likely to last.

Do you accept in-kind support?

Needs vary by programme and country. Please contact us before sending goods so we can make sure support reaches people safely and usefully.
